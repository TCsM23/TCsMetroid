About TCsMetroid

TCsMetroid is a workshop for Metroidvania maps. It treats a map as a graph: rooms are nodes, doors are edges, and every door has a rule that says which power-ups open it. Because the rules are data, the app can prove a map can be finished before anyone plays it.

Design

Drag rooms on a grid and draw doors between them. Give each door a rule such as J | (D & G).

Invent power-ups

Every map has its own power-up list: a letter, a name and a colour for each one.

Verify

The checker proves the boss can be reached, the route finder compares four searches, and the analysis finds traps.

Play

Press Play and explore it yourself. The game uses the same rules, so a locked door is locked everywhere.

  • Maps lists your maps and the templates you saved.
  • Sandbox is a scratch designer: nothing is saved until you ask.
  • Check, Route and Analysis are on every map's bar, under Verify.
  • Seed and Leaderboard shuffle the power-ups and rank every seed you make.

Algorithms inside

Parser
Recursive descent turns door rules into expression trees
BFS
Fixed-point check groups rooms into spheres of progress
Dijkstra · A*
Fastest route over (room, power-ups held) states
Kahn
Topological sort gives the order power-ups must be found
Tarjan
Strongly connected parts, split by one-way drops
Union-find
Regions a player can walk around freely
Reverse BFS
Soft-lock states the game warns you about
Fisher–Yates
Seeded shuffles for the randomizer

Words worth knowing

Sphere
Everything you can reach with what you hold now. Each new power-up opens the next sphere.
Soft-lock
A spot you can reach but never leave without the power-ups you need.
One-way door
A drop or shutter you can pass one way only.
Seed
A number that fixes one shuffle, so anyone can replay the same layout.
Par
The fewest moves the route finder needs to reach the boss.
